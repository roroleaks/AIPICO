# AIPICO 🩺🤖

> **AI-Powered Clinical Research & PICO Framework Assistant**  
> *Empowering evidence-based medicine, clinical research formulation, and structured literature synthesis.*

---

## 🌟 Overview

**AIPICO** is an advanced web-based clinical research tool designed to bridge the gap between artificial intelligence and evidence-based clinical practice. Built to streamline the rigorous process of scientific inquiry, AIPICO helps medical professionals, researchers, and educators seamlessly construct, refine, and execute clinical research questions using the internationally recognized **PICO** framework (**P**opulation/Patient, **I**ntervention, **C**omparison, **O**utcome).

Whether you are designing a systematic review, preparing a clinical trial protocol, or teaching evidence-based medicine, AIPICO accelerates the workflow from initial research question conceptualization to actionable search strategies.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **PICO Framework Architecture:** Automatically breaks down complex clinical scenarios into structured, searchable components.
*   **AI-Driven Research Synthesis:** Leverages modern LLM intelligence to suggest relevant clinical variables, endpoints, and methodology markers.
*   **Precision Query Generation:** Translates clinical questions into optimized search strings for medical databases (e.g., PubMed, Cochrane, MEDLINE).
*   **User-Centric Clinical Interface:** Clean, intuitive UI designed specifically for busy physicians, clinical researchers, and healthcare innovators.
*   **Institutional Foundation:** Developed in alignment with high standards of clinical utility and academic rigor, backed by the Egypt Health Foundation.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **Frontend / UI:** Responsive web architecture optimized for desktop and mobile clinical environments.
*   **Core Engine:** Advanced prompt workflows and algorithmic clinical parsing.
*   **Integration:** Designed to interface smoothly with modern research synthesis pipelines and reference tools.
